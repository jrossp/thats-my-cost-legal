# That's My Cost: MS Sortie Planner — Privacy Policy
*Last updated: July 28, 2026*

---

## 1. Who we are

"That's My Cost" ("the App") is an independent, unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with Bandai Namco Entertainment Inc. This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and how it's handled.

Contact for privacy questions: jrossp@hotmail.com

## 2. What we collect

| Data | Why we collect it | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Email address (or Apple/Google account identifier if you sign in that way) | Account creation, login, and account recovery | You, at signup |
| Optional display handle | Shown only if you choose to set one; otherwise your identity stays anonymized on all data you contribute | You, optional |
| Rotation reports you submit (queue, cost, environment, map, game type, timestamp) | The App's core function — community rotation tracking | You, when reporting |
| Device hash (a one-way anonymized device identifier) | Prevents a single person from operating multiple accounts to manipulate community consensus data | Generated automatically at signup |
| Preferred platform (PlayStation or Steam) | Shows you the right rotation board by default | You, at onboarding |
| Purchase receipt data | Verifying your one-time Premium purchase with Apple/Google | Apple App Store / Google Play, at time of purchase |
| Basic usage data (app crashes, general interaction analytics, if enabled) | Improving app stability and features | Automatically, if you haven't disabled it in your device settings |
| Advertising identifier (only if/when ads are introduced) | Serving non-personalized ads through an ad network | Automatically generated by your device, only if ads are active |

**We do not collect:** your real name (unless you choose to use it as a display handle), precise location, contacts, photos, or any Game account credentials. The App never asks for your Game (Bandai Namco) login and cannot access it.

## 3. What we never do

- We do not sell your data to third parties.
- We do not currently use your data for advertising. If the App introduces advertising in the future, this policy will be updated first, and any ads shown will be non-personalized (not based on tracking your individual activity) unless this policy is revised to say otherwise.
- We do not share your individual reports, email, or account activity with other users — other users only ever see an anonymized attribution (e.g. "Scout #4821"), never your email or account ID.
- We do not access your device's storage, contacts, camera, or location without a specific, separate feature request that would ask your permission at that time (no such feature currently exists in the App).

## 4. Who we share data with

- **Apple / Google**: to process purchases and verify your Premium status. Governed by their own respective privacy policies.
- **Our backend infrastructure provider (Supabase)**: hosts the database and authentication system that stores the data above, under its own data processing terms. Supabase does not use your data for its own purposes.
- **Push notification services (Apple/Google), if you enable alerts** (Premium feature): used solely to deliver the specific rotation alerts you configure.
- We do not share data with any other third party except where required by law.

## 5. Moderation and standing data

The App maintains an internal reputation score used to weigh the reliability of your reports. This score is never displayed to you or any other user, is used solely for consensus and anti-abuse purposes, and is not shared outside the App's own moderation system.

## 6. Your rights

- **Access & deletion**: you can delete your account at any time from Settings, which removes your login credentials and personal identifiers. Aggregated, anonymized rotation data you contributed may be retained as part of the historical community record, since it no longer identifies you once disassociated from your account.
- **Correction**: you can update your display handle or preferred platform at any time in Settings.
- **EU/UK/California residents**: depending on your location, you may have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA (e.g. the right to request a copy of your data, or to object to certain processing). Contact us at the email above to exercise these rights.

## 7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Data retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. Anonymized, aggregated rotation history may be retained indefinitely as part of the App's historical data, since it cannot be traced back to you once your account is deleted.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard practices (encrypted connections, access controls, row-level database security) to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to the App.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above, and continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 11. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this policy or your data: jrossp@hotmail.com
